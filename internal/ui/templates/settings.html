How this works

  • The page reads the same mailctl.yaml and the same environment the CLI does. Editing the config or the environment needs a restart of mailctl ui.
  • Plan and audit run only when you press the button, through the same engine as the CLI, so what you see here is what mailctl plan and mailctl audit print.
  • Nothing here applies. mailctl apply in a terminal makes changes, with its confirmation prompts and -prune gates intact.
  • The password and the activity log are the only things written to disk, under $XDG_DATA_HOME/mailctl (~/.local/share/mailctl when unset) or wherever -data points.
